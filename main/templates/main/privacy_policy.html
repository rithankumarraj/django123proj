Your information

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Information we handle

When you create an account or place an order, we handle the account details you provide, such as your username, email address and name, along with the items you order and the delivery address you enter. Your cart is stored in your session so it can be kept while you shop.

How we use it

We use this information to manage your account, keep your cart, prepare and deliver orders, show order and payment status, and respond to customer support requests.

Orders and payment information

Order records include products, quantities, totals, delivery details and payment status. The current site does not ask for or store full payment card details. Payment processing may change if a payment provider is added in the future; this policy should be updated if that happens.

Who can access order details

Order and delivery details are available to authorized store staff and delivery personnel so they can process and fulfil orders. We do not publish customer addresses or account details on the public storefront.

Sessions and security

The site uses session cookies for sign-in and cart functionality. Keep your sign-in credentials private and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission. We take reasonable steps to protect account and order information.

Access and questions

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact us at support@jcsd.com. We may need to retain some order information to complete transactions and meet applicable obligations.
